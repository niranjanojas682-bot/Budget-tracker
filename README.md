Key Features Included
Income and Expenses Tracking: Clearly separated with a live updating balance of the two.
Month Budget Tracking: Set your spending limit and watch the progress bar as it gets closer to the edge, turning yellow when you hit 80% of the budget, red when you go over the limit.
Transactions Filtering: Hide and show transactions categorized as Food, Utilities, Transport, Salary, etc.
Stored Locally: All transactions are saved in your browser local storage so that they persist between reloads and never get lost.
Sleek Design: Lightweight, easy to use, works on both mobile and desktop.
